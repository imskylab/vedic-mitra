Vedic Mitra

Your panchang, computed for your sky.

A modern, offline-first Android companion for Vedic timekeeping — panchanga, muhurta, Choghadiya, festivals and planetary positions, calculated for your exact location. No ads. No tracking. Open source.

What it does

Everything below is computed on-device for your coordinates — not looked up from a table.

🗓️

Full daily panchanga

Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, paksha, vara, ayana, ritu, maasa & samvatsara — named by the sunrise convention.

🕉️

Muhurta & kalams

Brahma & Abhijit Muhurta, Dur Muhurta, Varjyam, and the inauspicious Rahu, Yamaganda & Gulika kalams.

⏳

Choghadiya

All sixteen day and night Choghadiya windows, weekday-sequenced.

🌙

Sky & planets

Sunrise/sunset, moonrise/moonset, moon phase, golden hour, and the rashi of Sun, Moon, Guru & Shukra with next pravesh.

🪔

Festivals & vrats

Upcoming festivals, Ekadashi, Purnima, Amavasya, Sankashti Chaturthi, Pradosh, and Sankrantis.

🔔

Reminders that fire

Exact, reboot-survivable alarms for muhurta, Choghadiya and custom tithi targets, with flexible lead times.

Why it's different

The things paid panchang apps usually don't offer.

Computed, not looked up

Astronomy is derived from your coordinates using the Meeus ephemeris and Lahiri ayanamsa, so timings are right for where you actually are.

Offline & private

Core calculations run entirely on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking — your practice isn't the product.

Open source

AGPL-3.0, auditable and community-buildable. Cross-checked against drikpanchang.com and datepanchang.com before each release.

Vedic Mitra — under active development (pre-1.0). Feedback and contributions welcome.
