# Vedic Mitra 0.7.0

_Released 2026-08-22_

A shorter release than the last one, and a deliberate one. It adds five more hymns — including
Tulsidas's complete **Hanuman Chalisa** — and a new **Support** tab that makes it possible, for the
first time, to contribute to the project. Nothing has been taken away or put behind a payment:
every feature in Vedic Mitra remains free, ad-free, and tracker-free, and nothing on the Support
screen unlocks anything.

## ✨ New features

- **Support tab.** A fourth destination in the bottom bar, gathering the ways to keep Vedic Mitra
  going: a one-off or monthly contribution via GitHub Sponsors, a tip via Ko-fi, or a UPI ID you can
  copy straight into your payment app. Businesses that can't work within the app's open-source
  licence will find the commercial-licensing route here too. It also lists the ways to help that
  cost nothing — starring the repository, reporting a bug, reporting a panchanga timing that
  disagrees with your almanac, or contributing a translation.

  In keeping with the rest of the app, the screen does no networking of its own: links hand off to
  your browser, and the UPI ID is copied to your clipboard rather than opening a payment app, so it
  behaves sensibly on devices with no UPI app installed.

- **Five more stotras**, bringing the bundled library to **26**:
  - **Hanuman Chalisa** — Tulsidas's complete forty verses with the opening and closing dohas.
    Tuesday's "today's stotra" now suggests it.
  - **Lingashtakam** and **Nirvana Shatkam** (Shiva)
  - **Madhurashtakam** (Krishna)
  - **Gajananam Bhutaganadi** — the Ganesha dhyana verse

  Each in Devanagari with a line-for-line transliteration and a short note, as before.

## 💡 Improvements

- **Settings scrolls properly** on shorter screens, and its rows now share a common style with the
  About and Support screens, so the three read as one thing rather than three.

## 📄 Licensing & privacy

- **A privacy policy, in writing.** Vedic Mitra has always claimed no accounts, no analytics, no
  crash reporting and no ads. That claim now has a document behind it, spelling out exactly what is
  stored on your device, why each permission exists, and the single point at which the app touches
  the network — Android's own city-name lookup when you search for a location. See
  [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md).
- **Commercial licensing is a real offer.** The AGPL asks anything built on Vedic Mitra, hosted
  services included, to share its source in return. For teams that can't do that, there are now
  published tiers and prices and a contact address instead of "terms on request" — see
  [COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md). This changes nothing for anyone simply using
  the app, including professionally.
- **An explicit licensing exception** covering the app's use of Google Play services for optional
  location, so anyone redistributing or forking Vedic Mitra is unambiguously in the clear. See
  [LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md](../../LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md).

## 📦 Under the hood

- The Settings, About and Support screens were rebuilt on a single shared set of row components
  instead of each maintaining its own copy — less to go wrong as more settings arrive.
- Fixed the "Question / discussion" link in the issue templates, which pointed at a repository that
  doesn't exist.

---

**Upgrading:** no action needed — your saved profiles, locations, reminders, japa and meditation
history all carry over.
