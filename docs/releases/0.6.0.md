# Vedic Mitra 0.6.0

_Released 2026-08-19_

This release completes the hub — **every tile is now a working feature** — adds a new "tap to learn
what it means" layer across the astrology screens, and trims the app down for a leaner install. All the
astrology is still computed from real ephemeris data (validated against NASA JPL HORIZONS), not generic
text.

## ✨ New features

- **Rashifal — daily & weekly horoscope.** A real Moon-transit outlook for your sign: today's verdict
  plus the seven days ahead, graded by Chandrabala (the transit Moon's position from your sign). Read
  your own birth sign for a fully personalised outlook with Tarabala from your birth star, or tap any
  of the twelve rashis to browse it.
- **Kundali Matching (Guna Milan).** Pick a groom and a bride profile and see the classic 36-guna
  Ashtakoota compatibility across all eight kootas — Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana,
  Bhakoot and Nadi — with the total, the verdict, and any Nadi/Bhakoot dosha, scored from both Moons.
- **Japa — mala chant counter.** Tap the ring to count beads; a mala completes every 108 with a haptic
  buzz. Choose from a bundled catalog (Gayatri, Om Namah Shivaya, Mahamrityunjaya and the nine
  Navagraha beeja mantras, in Devanagari + transliteration). Your sittings are logged with a daily
  total and a streak, and an in-progress mala is saved so you can resume it.
- **Meditation — timed sit with a breath guide.** Pick a length (5–30 min) and settle in with a
  breathing circle that expands and contracts, plus a soft start/end bell. Each sit is logged with a
  daily total and a streak, and it surfaces today's **Brahma Muhurta** window with a "sit now"
  shortcut and an optional daily pre-dawn reminder.
- **Stotra — a library of hymns & shlokas.** 20+ traditional stotras (Ganesha, Shiva, Vishnu, Devi,
  the Navagraha stotra, the peace mantras and more), each in Devanagari with a transliteration and a
  short note. A "today's stotra" is suggested by the weekday, and the reader has an adjustable font
  size for chanting.

## 💡 Improvements

- **Tap to learn what a placement means.** Several screens now reveal a plain-language explanation when
  you tap:
  - **Kundali** — the ascendant sign, the Moon's nakshatra, your current mahadasha, and each graha's
    sign and house.
  - **Rashifal** — tap any day in the week strip for that day's full reading.
  - **Kundali Matching** — tap any of the eight guna rows to learn what that koota measures (the groom
    and bride pickers now also sit side by side).
- **New hub-tile artwork.** The shortcut tiles now carry hand-drawn mandala-style icons (a lotus
  zodiac wheel for Panchang, a mangal kalash for Muhurat, the twelve rashis for Rashifal, and more),
  enlarged to fill their chips.
- **Consistent, tidier selectors.** Every single-choice picker — profiles, rashis, mantras, sit
  length, search window, theme — is now a clean expandable dropdown instead of scrolling chip rows,
  scaling gracefully to long lists.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Reminders now actually notify.** Setting a reminder for an auspicious muhurta day (or a Brahma
  Muhurta meditation reminder) scheduled it, but the notification never appeared because the app hadn't
  asked for notification permission (default-denied on Android 13+). Those screens now request it, so
  reminders are delivered.

## 📦 Under the hood

- **Smaller app.** Release builds now run full code + resource shrinking (R8), ship only the resources
  the app needs, and dropped a large unused icon library — for a noticeably leaner install. Install the
  Play App Bundle build for per-device downloads.

---

**Upgrading:** no action needed — your saved profiles, locations, and reminders carry over.
