# Vedic Mitra 0.6.1

_Released 2026-08-19_

A small patch release that fixes a crash introduced in 0.6.0.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Fixed a crash when choosing a birthplace.** Adding or editing a profile and picking a place from
  the search results could crash the app. The offline time-zone lookup now fails gracefully — falling
  back to a longitude-based estimate if it can't resolve the exact zone — so selecting a birthplace is
  reliable again, and the resolved time zone is still correct.

---

**Upgrading:** no action needed — your saved profiles, locations, and reminders carry over. If a
profile you added under 0.6.0 is missing its birthplace, just open it and pick the place again.
