# Vedic Mitra 0.8.0

_Released 2026-08-26_

The astrology release. 0.7.0 added ways to support the project; this one adds the thing the project
is for. A birth chart in Vedic Mitra now carries seventeen divisional charts, three dasha systems
read three levels deep, ashtakavarga, and a matchmaking screen that answers the question people
actually ask before a marriage — and every one of those was checked against an independent
implementation before it shipped.

Two things in here **change numbers you may have seen before**. Read those sections if you have
looked at your dasha dates or your kundali already.

## ✨ New features

### Divisional charts — seventeen of them

The navamsha turned out not to be a special case. Counting divisions continuously from 0° Mesha
reproduces the classical rule for a whole family of charts, and where it does not, a twelve-entry
table finishes the job. The Charts section of the Kundali now holds the lagna figure, the same
placements read from the Moon, and sixteen vargas behind one chip row — D-3 (siblings), D-4
(property), D-9 (marriage), D-10 (career), D-12 (parents), D-60, and the rest.

Each says what it is traditionally read for, and how finely it cuts a sign.

### Three dasha systems, three levels deep

Vimshottari, **Ashtottari** and **Yogini**, each running mahadasha → antardasha →
**pratyantardasha**. The period you are in and the period inside it now sit on one page instead of
two, under a system selector.

### Ashtakavarga

Bindus per sign for each graha, and the sarvashtakavarga that sums them — always 337 across the
twelve signs. A transit through a sign holding 30 bindus reads very differently from the same
transit through one holding 20, which is what makes this worth having.

### Mangal dosha, and the four additional porutham

Across much of India, Mangal dosha (Manglik, Kuja dosha, Chevvai dosham) is the **first** question
asked of a proposed match, and the app could not answer it. It can now — with its classical
cancellations, because a dosha reported without them is worse than none: most charts trigger the
rule somewhere, and a bare "Manglik" on nearly everyone is alarming and useless.

Alongside it, **Mahendra, Vedha, Rajju and Sthree Dheerga** — the four pass-or-fail conditions read
beside the thirty-six gunas. They are deliberately kept out of the guna total: a strong score should
not be able to bury a failed Rajju.

Every one shows its working — the count between two nakshatras, which limbs they fall on, which
placement raised the dosha and what cancels it — because these are precisely the rules where
almanacs disagree, and a verdict you cannot check against your own source is worth very little.

### Also new

- **Jataka properties** — varna, vashya, yoni, gana, nadi, the samvats and the ayanamsa at birth.
- **Spashta Graha** — every graha's position to the arcminute, with retrograde and combustion marked.
- **Pramukh Yoga** — the named combinations present in the chart.
- **Graha drishti** — whole-sign Parashari aspects.
- **"Ends in" for every limb** of the panchanga, not just the tithi.

## 🔁 Changed

### Your dasha dates have moved

A dasha year is the **sidereal** year, 365.2564 days. It was the Julian year, 365.25. That is about
six hours per century of elapsed timeline and reaches **roughly eighteen hours** by the end of the
120-year cycle, so periods late in your chart will have shifted by up to a day.

This was found by fitting the constant against 729 published period boundaries rather than assuming
it: the worst disagreement was 66,255 seconds with the old value and 604 with the new one. Some
traditions intend a 360-day year, which is a different timeline altogether rather than a refinement
of this one; the app follows the sidereal year, consistent with computing everything else from real
positions.

### The Kundali screen is six sections, not ten pages

It had grown a page at a time until reaching the last one meant swiping past every other, guided by
ten identical dots. It is now **Charts · Jataka · Grahas · Yogas · Dasha · Reading**, with named
tabs, grouped by the question you are asking rather than by which calculation produced the answer.

"Details" became **Reading** — it is the one section that says what any of this is taken to mean,
and it was named as an afterthought and left last.

### Kundali Matching is one surface

Gunas, conditions and Mangal dosha were three stacked cards, which put eight koota rows above
everything capable of vetoing a match. Now: the score, then anything wrong with it, then the
breakdown.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Location no longer sticks on New Delhi** after you switch location on and come back. Two causes:
  the system reports no cached position when location has been off, and the home screen only ever
  resolved once per launch. It now asks for a fresh fix and reloads whenever you return to the app —
  which also means leaving the app open overnight no longer shows you yesterday's panchang.
- **The home card leads with the tithi that is actually running.** The day is still named by its
  sunrise tithi, as every published panchanga does, but a card read at nine in the evening was
  headlining a tithi that ended before breakfast. It now shows what is running and when the previous
  one gave way.
- **Pada was wrong at its boundaries**, and seven yogas that did not survive checking against an
  independent implementation were removed rather than left in.

## 📋 Notes

- **Houses are whole-sign throughout.** Degree-based bhava chalit cusps are not computed, so a chart
  drawn with cusps will place some grahas in a different house. That is a different house system,
  not a disagreement.
- **The finest vargas are near this engine's limit.** A D-60 division is 30 arcminutes wide and
  planetary positions are good to about an arcminute, so roughly one placement in twelve sits close
  enough to a division edge to fall either way. Below about D-24 your **birth time** matters more
  than the arithmetic: the ascendant moves a degree in four minutes.
- **Six divisional charts are deliberately absent** — D-2, D-5, D-30, D-81, D-108 and D-144 do not
  follow the general rule and would have to be approximated, which is not worth doing.
- **One known divergence.** For a Moon in the last two or first two nakshatras, Ashtottari period
  *boundaries* follow this app's own uniform rule rather than the reference's. The ruling lord is
  unaffected.
- Still no ads, no tracking, no accounts, and no network calls. Everything above is computed on your
  device.
